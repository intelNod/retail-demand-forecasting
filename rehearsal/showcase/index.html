01 · Problem

Inventory planners need a defensible estimate, not an automatic order.

USER

Inventory planner

Reviews product-store demand before deciding how much stock to prepare.

INPUT

Known before the week

Completed sales history, planned prices, dates, events, and SNAP information.

OUTPUT

Weekly units

A non-negative forecast used as decision support with human review.

02 · Pipeline

Raw daily files become leakage-safe weekly features.

01 · AUDIT

Inspect five M5 files

Check identifiers, dates, missing values, duplicates, prices, and sales ranges.

02 · PREPARE

Wide to long

Pandas melt converts d_1…d_1941 columns and combines them with calendar and prices.

03 · PROTECT

Split by time

Train comes before validation and protected test; rolling sales use shift(1).

04 · PREDICT

Run the final model

Twenty numeric features enter the frozen Random Forest and produce weekly demand.

Known limitation

M5 represents historical Walmart stores in three US states, not Uzbekistan. High-demand weeks are harder: rows with 21+ actual units have MAE 12.33 and average underprediction of 5.10 units. The model supports a planner; it does not place orders automatically.
